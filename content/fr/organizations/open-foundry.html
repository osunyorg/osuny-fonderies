Open Foundry is a free platform for curated open-source fonts; to highlight their beauty, promote their creators and encourage exploration. Created almost a decade ago, open-foundry.com has been one of the first places on the web dedicated to high-quality open source typefaces. Now in its second iteration, with its core rebuilt, the project is ready to extend its catalogue with new discoveries, highlighting their makers.
